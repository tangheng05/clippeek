# clippeek

Screenshots straight to your clipboard and your own Zipline server, from the menu bar.

Take a screenshot with ⇧⌘3, ⇧⌘4 or ⇧⌘5 as usual. clippeek puts it on the clipboard so you can paste it
right away, uploads it to your self-hosted [Zipline](https://github.com/diced/zipline) server, and
removes the file from your Mac. A short history in the menu bar copies any recent screenshot again with
one click.

Status: in development.

Needs macOS 26 or later.
